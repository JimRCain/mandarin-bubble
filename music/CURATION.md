## The two rules that came out of this

**1. Pipa on the lead line.** Across both batches, every track with `pipalead` on the
lead was liked (5/5); every track with anything else on the lead graded lower
(erhu *meh*, zheng *ok*, dizi *good*/*busy*). This is **timbre, not melody** — the
lead line is generated by `gen_phrase(rng, ..., feel, lo, hi, lead_oct, ...)` and the
voice name is never passed to it, so two pieces with the same seed/feel/mode get an
identical contour in a different instrument. It also matches his independent pick in
the soundfont shootout: he called DSK's PIPA "the most Chinese to my western ear".

**2. Never pipa on both lead and counter.** `bell-tower` is the only track with
`lead="pipalead"` *and* `counter="pipalead"`, and it's the only one he called busy —
it doubles the brightest texture. Use dizi (airy) or zheng (plucked, darker) as the
counter instead. Batch 2 follows this.

## What did NOT hold up

- **Density is not the problem.** "Busy" does not track notes/sec: bell-tower is
  5.84 n/s while four liked tracks sit above it (firefly-lane 7.22, spice-road 7.18,
  night-market 6.56, jasmine-court 6.07). The two densest tracks in the set are both
  favourites. Do not thin out melodies to chase "busy".
- **Slow and 3/4 lost.** He picked the bright-mode 4/4 mid-tempo lane (88-132 bpm)
  and ignored the 3/4 pieces (river-ferry, market-morning) and everything under
  90 bpm (tea-terrace, incense-clock, rain-on-tiles, starlit-pool). Half the
  original ten were built outside that lane on purpose, to span five modes and
  three meters as a variation exercise.

## Rule 3: the recipe space is exhausted — vary (feel, texture), not just tempo

He called `opera-mask` "quite similar to previous songs", and he is right in a way
that is measurable: it is **徵 + driving + wide — the identical `(mode, feel,
texture)` recipe as `night-market`**, which he liked. Only tempo (130 vs 120) and
seed differ. Batch 2 leaned on a narrow slice of the axes and the collisions show:

    (gong, driving, rising)  firefly-lane(132) jasmine-court(112) dragon-boat(128)
    (gong, driving, wide)    courtyard-rain(96) pagoda-steps(116)
    (gong, flowing, broken)  bamboo-grove(88) lantern-river(92) willow-walk(90)
    (zhi, driving, rising)   spice-road(132) teahouse(108)
    (zhi, driving, wide)     night-market(120) stone-lane(100) opera-mask(130)
    (zhi, flowing, broken)   bell-tower(124) lotus-pond(118)

`feel` has 5 values and `texture` 4, so there is room — but future batches must pick
a `(mode, feel, texture)` combination that is not already taken, and should vary
`lead_oct` and the counter voice too. Do not treat a new tempo as a new piece.

A melodic-similarity metric was attempted and **abandoned as too weak to trust**:
cosine over a 13-bin interval histogram scored every pair under 0.19, including
pairs that sound obviously alike, so it has no discriminating power at this size.
The recipe table above is the reliable check.

## The 二泉映月 lane (Marie's suggestion)

Marie suggested 二泉映月 (Abing's "Moon Reflected on Second Spring", 1950). Three
pieces were written to that idiom — `moon-well` (徵 56), `still-water` (羽 52),
`deep-spring` (商 60) — all with erhu on the lead at `lead_oct=4` (62–84, well below
the pipa leads at 79–96) and a sparse or flowing feel.

This deliberately breaks Jim's own lane, so treat it as an exception, not the new
default. **The slides (滑音) and vibrato that carry most of the real expression are
not implemented** — MIDI needs pitchwheel events and `render.py` emits none. That is
the next lever if these land.

**Resolved (Jim, same day): the direction stays.** The 二泉映月 lane is dropped —
"no, let's keep our current direction". The three pieces remain on disk and on the
engine, but the bright pipa lane is the direction. Do not reopen this.

## Full songs

Jim's verdict: **"Ok, that's a winner."** The 80-bar form is approved — use it for
any loop promoted to a song.

Both songs were loop recipes (16 bars); they are now structured pieces in
`tools/full_song.py`, 80 bars each:

    0  intro    8   guzheng + bass, sparse, no melody
    1  A       16   pulse + bass + pipa lead
    2  A2      16   + trem intensity layer
    3  B       16   bridge, modulating a FIFTH UP, counter enters, trem drops
    4  A3      16   home key, trem back, counter doubled, lead range widened
    5  outro    8   thins to guzheng + bass + a lead cadencing on the tonic

The bridge is a real modulation, not a texture change: `bridge_mode()` shifts the
mode +3 in MODE_NAMES, which is a fifth up (宫→徵, 徵→商) — the classic 起承转合 转.

Verified the form is audible rather than assuming it, by measuring RMS per section:

    dragon-boat  intro .053 | A .090 | A+trem .097 | B .076 | A3 .097 | outro .063
    spice-road   intro .044 | A .078 | A+trem .088 | B .080 | A3 .093 | outro .055

Both dip at the bridge and peak at A3, as intended.

**Pitfall — a loop may be cut dead at the barline, a song may not.** Trimming at the
barline left the release clipped and the last 0.5s sitting at 0.06–0.08 RMS: an
audible chop at the end. `render_songs.py` keeps a 2.5 s `TAIL`. Check the tail
RMS is ~0.0000 after any change to the render length.

### The eight keepers, expanded

Jim approved the form ("Ok, that's a winner") and the batch ("Yes, you are good to
go"). All eight remaining keepers are now full songs in the same 80-bar form,
recipes copied verbatim from `variations.py` so these are the loops he graded, only
expanded. Sequenced by ascending tempo so no two neighbours share a groove:

    0:00  bamboo-grove   宫 gong  88   3:41
    3:41  willow-walk    宫 gong  90   3:36
    7:16  lantern-river  宫 gong  92   3:31

   13:48  jasmine-court  宫 gong 112   2:54
   16:42  pagoda-steps   宫 gong 116   2:48
   19:30  night-market   徵 zhi  120   2:43
   22:12  firefly-lane   宫 gong 132   2:28
   24:40  end

`bamboo-grove` is the one keeper with dizi on the LEAD, so it needs `lead_oct=6`
(the global `DIZI_TRANSPOSE` -24 then lands it at 60-81; `lead_oct=5` would drop it
to C3). Its counter is `pipalead`, the same layout as the cut `bell-tower`.

**Delivery pitfall — the batch is 24:40, which is 20.7 MiB at the 112k preview
bitrate and risks an iLink throttle.** `make_album.py` takes the delivery bitrate as
an argument; the batch went out at 64k (11.3 MiB, 11.8 MB). The 112k previews and
the OGG game assets stay on disk as the reference. Game assets total ~7.2 MiB for
all eight OGGs.
